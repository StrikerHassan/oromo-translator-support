Oromo Translator Privacy Policy

Translate. Understand. Connect.

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Oromo Translator is an English ↔ Oromo translation application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the app.

Information We Process

Oromo Translator processes text that you enter for translation. Translation text is transmitted to our server and translation service providers as necessary to provide translation results.

If you use the voice input feature, the app processes an audio recording of your speech so that it can be converted into text. Audio is transmitted to our server and speech-processing service provider to perform speech-to-text transcription.

When you use text-to-speech features, text may be transmitted to our server and speech service provider in order to generate audio playback.

How Information Is Used

Information processed by Oromo Translator is used to provide the app's translation, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech functionality, as well as to operate and improve the reliability of those features.

Service Providers

Oromo Translator relies on third-party infrastructure and service providers to perform functions such as translation, speech recognition, text-to-speech generation, and application hosting. Information necessary to perform these functions may be transmitted to those providers for processing.

Temporary Processing and Caching

Some translation and text-to-speech results may be temporarily cached by our server to improve performance and reduce repeated processing. Voice recordings may also be temporarily processed on our server as part of speech-to-text functionality.

Accounts and Advertising

The current version of Oromo Translator does not require users to create an account and does not contain third-party advertising or advertising tracking.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Oromo Translator. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Oromo Translator does not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles or serving targeted advertising.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Oromo Translator develops or as its features and services change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
